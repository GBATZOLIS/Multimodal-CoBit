\textbf{Continuous bitstream diffusion and entropy-based scheduling.} Diffusion models for discrete data commonly use categorical corruption or apply Gaussian Diffusion to embeded discrete variables in continuous space \citep{austin2021structured, sahoo2024simple, li2022diffusionlm, dieleman2022continuous}. Analog Bits \citep{chen2023analog} represents discrete values directly as binary string with continuously diffused bits, approach which has been extended to language modeling by CoBit \citep{method:cobit}. Multimodal diffusion and flow models have meanwhile explored joint and conditional generation across image--text and protein sequence--structure modalities \citep{bao2023unidiffuser,tang2023codi,campbell2024multiflow,wang2024dplm}.

More recently, Trentini et al.~\citep{trentini2026} generalized this information-theoretic perspective beyond diffusion, deriving entropy-rate schedulers for flow-matching and Schrödinger-bridge generative models.

\textbf{Information-theoretic diffusion scheduling.} Entropic time scheduling for diffusion models was first introduced by \citep{stancevic2025entropic}, allocating sampling steps according to the rate at which information is lost along the diffusion process. \citep{raya2026infonoise} subsequently used related entropy-rate quantities to allocate noise levels during training. More recently, \citep{trentini2026entropybridgeconditionalmarginaldiscretization} generalized this information-theoretic perspective beyond diffusion, deriving entropy-rate schedulers for flow and bridge generative models.
